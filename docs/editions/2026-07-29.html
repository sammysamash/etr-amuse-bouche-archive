Imports, Exports & Closings + A New (York) Deli
July 29, 2026 • Vol 1, Issue 26

the LEFTOVERS
🇪🇸 Hey! Did you read the very special, breaking news edition of this newsletter, delivered direct to your in-box last night? We still can’t believe Valencian Gold is (a) coming back, to (b) the Arts District. 🥳 If you missed this big ETR exclusive, catch up here.
​
​​🍊 Las Vegas is accustomed to welcoming popular and-or trendy restaurants and bars from other cities. But the number of notable Las Vegas exports is surprisingly thin: Golden Steer, Double Down Saloon, Tacotarian, Sugar Factory… 🤔. Thankfully, Roy Choi is about to add to the list. The SoCal chef is taking his original Park MGM eatery Best Friend to the new OCVibe in Anaheim. In a parallel to Park MGM, OCVibe is a $4 billion, 100-acre master-planned sports and entertainment district around the Honda Center. Choi, who grew up in Orange County, says that north OC culture will “be in the DNA” of the new spot, celebrating the burger stands 🍔 and taquerias 🌮 rooted in the region, while incorporating design inspiration from ska 🏁, punk, BMX and skateboarding.
​
🍻 To maintain balance in the cosmos, Las Vegas will inherit yet another SoCal export in September, when Finney’s Crafthouse opens a 17th location (its first outside California) in Downtown Summerlin. The sports-focused “neighborhood tavern” reinvigorates the vacant Wolfgang Puck Players Locker, which closed in 2023. Notably, the location, first opened as Puck’s Bar & Grill, was at one time slated to become Puck’s Lupo, after that Italian restaurant closed in Mandalay Bay. It never happened. A second LV Finney's arrives later in 2026 in Town Square, confirming the chain’s commitment to Las Vegas malls doing an impression of a neighborhood.
​
🍕 Does DTLV need more pizza? How about a New York-style Italian deli that serves pizza? Now you have our attention. With a grand opening party coming August 15 (soft opening: Aug. 3), Slice Deli is setting up shop in the new “mini container park” on Commerce St at Wyoming Ave. Yes, Slice has pizza, but it is the hero menu that intrigues us — especially since losing District Deli & Coffee on Main Street. 😢
​
🇫🇷 Bar Boheme is steadily stepping up its game, bringing a joie de vivre to the Arts District. The seating is improved, the kitchen has found its rhythm, and service is catching up (though the bar has never needed the help). ETR fans know Boheme recently added a wonderful bartop happy hour (4–6pm), a baked goods sale (monthly on the third Saturday, 8am), and a brunch that has finally outgrown its Sunday-only origins. Now, it’s a true weekend affair, serving Saturday and Sunday (10am–3pm), with the kind of menu that swings heavy or light, hot or chilled, a la carte or prix fixe — all depending on your brunching 🥂 style.
​
🇹🇭  A new project from the folks behind Weera Thai has quietly opened in southwest Las Vegas, featuring a mash-up of northern Thai cooking and the grilled meats of a Japanese Izakaya. Kinzuu Izakaya has taken two low-key months perfecting their food and service, and is now ready for a grand opening this Monday, August 3. There will be food and drink deals, but we recommend avoiding the rush by visiting this weekend.
 

SPILLED TEA 

ETR fans know downtown’s ground-breaking Andre's French Restaurant on 6th St. would be reborn (after an almost 30 year vacancy) as an outpost of the French Riviera Tropézienne Bakery. Come Friday that day will arrive — sort of. The local bakery and cafe will begin serving coffee ☕ and baked goods 🥐 July 31, with full bistro service slated for September…

On a recent ETR episode, Ash wondered, “Has the Arts District reached peak bar?” While new bars are being developed, there have also been some notable recent closures. In December 2025, HUDL Brewing Company shuttered after a 5-year run. Then in February, bye-bye Berlin Bar. Now, Bad Beat Brewing announced they will close after service on August 2.

Bad Beat was in Henderson from 2014–2024, but closed for an extended period before their September 2025 Arts District debut. That puts their DTLV tenure at a brief 11 months 🤯. Similar to HUDL, Bad Beat has no kitchen, making us wonder: is food a prerequisite to a successful beer bar in the Arts District? Or is this part of an overall decline in beer consumption? 🤔

Happy hours are so… 2026. Everyone, it seems, is looking for a reason to go out but not drop a car payment on dinner. Ole Manny’s is the latest option in the Arts District, running Tue–Thu (3–6pm) and Fri (3–5pm 😠), offering three street 🌮 ($14) or two 🐟 tacos and a 🍺 ($15). We’ll take option two…

ORDER OF THE WEEK
New School Chicken Parm Sando
Esther’s Kitchen​
​
Faithful podcast listeners (and Amuse-Bouche readers) know John has an issue with chicken parm — unless it comes out of Nicole Brisson’s kitchen 🧐. Ash holds no such grudge, so when she does lunch at Esther's Kitchen, the New School Chicken Parm Sando is a go-to.
​
Starting with a bountiful breaded chicken cutlet that barely fits its sesame roll, the sandwich is deliciously dressed with vodka sauce, a spread of pistachio pesto, a cheese-pull portion of basil ricotta, and peppery arugula. House made chips? Naturally, on the side.
​
Available lunch and brunch only, it’s a full day's eating on a bun. Split it with a friend, or wrap half for later. Your 3 p.m. self will thank you.
