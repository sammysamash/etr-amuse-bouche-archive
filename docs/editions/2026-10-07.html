the LEFTOVERS 🥡
👻 With October comes spooky season fun and a handful of themed bar pop-ups — like this one, this one, and this one. This year, something new has piqued our interest: A Night of Poe. This “hauntingly immersive” event blends cocktail pairings 🍸 with Edgar Allen Poe’s genuinely creepy writing 🫣, using live theater, storytelling, and music to weave the scary narratives. Honestly, this might be the thing that gets us back to Area15.
— ✦ —
🧀​Artisan cheese and charcuterie shop Cured & Whey is celebrating its 10th anniversary with an Oktoberfest party October 17. The $35 admission gets you 🇩🇪​ German-inspired food, a (hopefully) cold beer, live music, plus 20% off any in-store purchases. Call 702-429-3617 to get your ticket.
— ✦ —
🐒 East Fremont’s churn is real, and one spot has hit reinvention #3. Opened in 2013 as the ambitious Mexican eatery La Comida (from longtime restaurateurs Michael and Jenna Morton), the space was taken over in 2022 by Ryan Doherty’s Corner Bar Management, which flipped it to Mexican dining concept La Mona Rosa. Recently, La Mona Rosa leaned more into partying than eating, with raucous brunches and late night DJ events. Now, La Mona Rosa has flipped to Pink Monkey, a full-on Latin/Hip-Hop nightclub. The switch further reinforces the Bourbon Street vibe of East Fremont, while mostly leaving the foodie scene to the Arts District and Chinatown… and we’re okay with that.
— ✦ —
☹️ It’s never good news when a restaurant closes, especially when it closes suddenly. That's what happened to La Neta Cocina y Lounge in Downtown Summerlin last Tuesday. For five years, La Neta boasted an inviting bar and lounge that opened to the outdoors, offering a vibey happy hour respite from many of the mall’s other offerings. At least one of our staffers is pouring out a Spicy Piña for them.
— ✦ —
😠 Just after we praised Station Casinos for bringing local brands to their resorts, news of three local storefronts closing at Durango​ drops. Both Vesta Coffee locations (lobby and food court) will shutter Nov 10, as well as Yu Or Mi Sushi (which has also closed its Arts District spot). Given the coffee lineup at other Station Casinos, we’ll guess that Starbucks is coming soon. As to what will replace Yu Or Mi… 🤔
— ✦ —
⛳ Some good news: the Cliff House Grill overlooking the Wynn’s 18th hole and it’s gorgeous waterfall is now open. Not to be confused with Wynn’s old Country Club (now Sartiano's and Zero Bond), the restaurant is part of a newly-built clubhouse integrated into the golf course, offering dining room, patio and bar seating with waterfall and Sphere views.
— ✦ —
🏆 “We’d give 6 stars if we could.”​ Love Yelp or hate it, we won’t dismiss the unsurprising news that it just named Bar Boheme one of its 25 top-rated new restaurants in the U.S. Also unsurprising? Yelp says “breakfast and brunch” is the most searched dining in every food-loving city. Apparently, at any given time, “bottomless mimosa” is being typed into a search bar…

SPILLED TEA 🫖

🥪 Is the former District Deli space in the Arts District prepping for a comeback? Whispers about a potential reboot have circulated, and a recent weeknight walk-by revealed the interior lights lit for the first time since District Deli closed in January. No licenses have been issued or permits pulled, and the space still looks as if it was hastily abandoned during an alien invasion 🤔. While we’ve moved on to Slice, we’ll keep watching 👀.
— ✦ —

☕ The fight between 7 Brew and Dutch Bros over the closed Salad and Go drive-thrus is almost over. 7 Brew won the war, gaining control of 63 locations, including three in Las Vegas (Durango Dr, Fort Apache Rd, Silverado Ranch Blvd). The other four in LV remain up for grabs, and bidders include Dutch Bros, “dirty soda” seller Swig, and… 7 Brew.
— ✦ —

☕☕ Coffee is on fire — and it will only burn hotter as we slide into cooler weather. Here, then, is the ETR-approved Las Vegas Vernacular lesson you never knew you needed. In Vegas, a coffee shop is a diner, like Lou’s, the ​Peppermill, or the mostly-gone 24/7 casino diners of Lost Vegas. A café is what mostly exists today as a polished, espresso-focused, fast-casual eatery (Vesta, Torino, Sunrise). And a coffeehouse? Think Beatnik vibes​— open mic, spoken word, live music, late hours. The last true coffeehouse Las Vegas had was The Beat (back when East Fremont was new), but The Griffin is giving it a go​ during the day.
— ✦ —

☕☕☕ Speaking of coffee… following a cosmetic overhaul of the former Dig It! Coffee space in the Arts District, Almond & Oat has targeted Wednesday, October 7 as the soft-open date for its Downtown café.
— ✦ —

🤯 There’s an IHOP with a limited-menu drive-thru at 1824 E. Lake Mead Blvd. That’s it; that’s the tea.

ORDER OF THE WEEK
Texas Twinkies
Soulbelly BBQ
​
It’s hard to believe, but football season is well underway. And you know what goes best with it? Jalapeño poppers. Okay, beer. But also jalapeño poppers. Now the popper has a bigger, brawnier Lone Star cousin: the Texas Twinkie. Made famous by Hurtado Barbecue in Arlington, Texas, it has two-stepped its way to Las Vegas and onto the menu at Soulbelly BBQ.
​
Take a jumbo jalapeño, stuff it with cream cheese and smoked brisket trimmings, wrap it in bacon (because of course), smoke it until the bacon crisps, and finish with a drizzle of barbecue sauce. Touchdown!
​
One is basically a small meal, but who stops at one?
