Global Cuisine + Buffet Revival?
October 14, 2026 • Vol 1, Issue 37

the LEFTOVERS 🥡
🧆 A popular Lebanese restaurant has reappeared in Las Vegas after a lengthy absence. Hedary’s first opened in 2004 in the former Bergin Baking Co. & Cafe spot on W. Sahara Ave, before closing around 2015. Now, Hedary’s has been resurrected by Mariz Khoury (sister of founder Frank Hedary, and herself founder of Koury’s Mediterranean) at the Gramercy, where ETR fave Anima lives. The restaurant stays true to their roots, using old family recipes for the frarej, baba ghanoush, ftayir, and more.
— ✦ —
🍽️ With the news that Souplantation is making a California comeback after shuttering during COVID, could Las Vegas — always keen to capitalize 🤑 on a trend — retreat from often-pricey food halls to the (perceived) bargain of the AYCE buffet? The city is currently down from more than 50 buffets pre-shutdown to only 12 today — and rumors say more closures are coming. Five of the remaining 12 are at splurge-worthy luxury properties, while four are at value-oriented casinos, meaning there’s plenty of room for a mid-market revival of these cost-conscious gorge-fests at a moment when visitors would likely… eat them up. 🙄
— ✦ —
🐟 When it comes to the Vegas-essential power meal, the three-course prix fixe Business Lunch at Estiatorio Milos has long been a fave — so long that it once cost $20.12 (in 2012…). Now priced at $45, it tracks well ahead of inflation, but value remains. The regular Biz Lunch menu includes octopus, a sushi-grade tuna burger, and roasted organic chicken breast. But if you make your three selections from the “Quick & Delicious” menu, you are guaranteed to be gone in 60 minutes or it’s free 🤯. Now if only we could get them to include three martinis​🍸 …
— ✦ —
🇵🇪 It wasn’t that long ago (to some of us…) that Las Vegas fine dining was defined by casino-run “gourmet rooms,” and later, celebrity chefs. One upside of the post-COVID era? Our dining scene is evolving fast, with a wave of fancy global-cuisine eateries — Gymkhana, Maroon, Stubborn Seed, Cote — landing here. And soon, joining Cleaver and Braseria at The Collective east of the Strip, is Ayni Peruvian Restaurant. Details are light, but the name comes from the Andean concept of reciprocity. The website waxes poetic that “every ingredient that reaches our table comes from a seed that trusted the sun.” We’ll trust them, and report back once doors open later this fall.

SPILLED TEA 🫖

👷 At the historic Hard Hat Lounge — where the building dates to 1962, the bar to 1970 — an big party October 16th will celebrate the 4th Anniversary of its rebirth, following a restoration and reopening by Las Vegas native rock star Frank Sidoris. If previous Hard Hat parties are any indication, expect crowds for live music (with special guests), DJs, and resident Stay Tuned Burgers cranking out the goods as fast as they can.
— ✦ —

👨‍🍳 While on one of our infrequent post-work booze crawls, we ran into Esther’s Kitchen Beverage Director Tucker St. John, who promptly invited us to sister-spot Petite Boheme’s weekly celebration of those who keep our F&B scene humming. Running every Friday night through October, Love The Industry (LIT) features drink and food specials, including a deliciously sloppy new burger that mimics a famous chain’s 🤠🍔🥓🧅 cheeseburger, plus jalapeño queso fries, which we can attest are 🔥. You don’t have to show your TAM or health card to enjoy LIT, but appreciate those who do!
— ✦ —

🌯 Does the Arts District need more Mexican food in the morning? Apparently, we are about to find out, as whispers suggest that the “lights on” we reported at Main Street’s long-closed District Deli are the early signs of a new restaurant focusing on breakfast burritos. With sit-down Mexican brekkie available from Mi Barrio (9am), Casa Don Juan (10am weekdays, 8am weekends), and nearby Doña Maria’s (8am), maybe a fast-casual burrito joint for those rushing to work is what’s missing. 🤔

ORDER OF THE WEEK
1/4-Sheet Buffalo Style Italian Sausage Pie
Naked City Pizza
​
With the UNLV Runnin’ Rebels 🏀 tipping off their season with an exhibition loss last week 😞,​ it’s a fine time to​ drop into the venerable Office Bar. Within walking distance of the Thomas & Mack, and on ETR’s recent list of 50 Essential Bars, the Office Bar has a secret weapon: Chris Palmieri’s neighboring Buffalo-faithful Naked City Pizza ​serves as the bar’s kitchen.
​
For the uninitiated, Buffalo-style sheet pie may look like a Detroit square, but it eats differently: a medium-thick, pan-crisped crust (instead of a deep, airy one), a sweet oregano-loaded sauce tucked under the cheese, and toppings running right to the edge (great for those who refuse to eat “the bones”). Add tasty, sage-forward Italian sausage or cup-and-char pepperoni, or go for the insanely good Guinea Pie (white garlic sauce, fresh spinach, “Grandma’s meatballs,” mozzarella, and ricotta). Win or lose, it’s the ultimate pre- or post-game play.
