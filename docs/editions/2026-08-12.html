Goodbye, Salad and Go • Hello, R&G Lounge
August 12, 2026 • Vol 1, Issue 28

the LEFTOVERS
🦀 R&G Lounge — a 41-year Cantonese institution of San Francisco’s Chinatown — is headed to Las Vegas and the Forum Shops this year. R&G blew up when Anthony Bourdain stopped by with No Reservations to enjoy the house-specialty salt-and-pepper Dungeness crab: a whole live crab battered, deep-fried, and tossed with Chinese salt-and-pepper seasoning. Expect it to be front and center at the Strip location.
​
🥬 We don’t know who thought it would be a great idea to sell salads from a drive-thru (if McDonald’s failed at it…), but in a surprise to almost nobody, Salad and Go declared bankruptcy and closed all stores last week. Moving quickly to scoop up most of their locations? Two rival drive-thru coffee chains — Dutch Bros and 7Brew — are in a battle for control of the stores. Plenty of people are apparently perfectly willing to idle in a drive-thru line for 20 minutes for hi-cal coffee drinks… but not for kale.
​
🍰 Speaking of interesting drive-thru ideas, Shook Shakery opens the first of its four Las Vegas area locations August 29 in Henderson. The new concept offers one product — milkshakes mixed with cake — and is reportedly spending $12 million on the local stores. We wish the locally-born concept nothing but the best, of course, while wondering if Dutch Bros or 7Brew have quietly put these spots on their radar.
​
🥩 Some fret about the post-COVID disappearance of the Las Vegas buffet. While we get it (sorta), we’re heartbroken about the slow death of the coffee shop. Once a casino staple, the coffee shop (AKA, diner) was typically running 24/7/365, and (back in the day) selling 49¢ breakfasts, $4.99 platters of prime rib, and priceless Vegas stories. The most recent to call it quits? Siegel’s 1941 in downtown’s El Cortez closed in April, soon to be replaced by Alex Prime, a dinner-only ​New York style steakhouse.
 

SPILLED TEA 

🍽️ The folks behind Vegas Unstripped (one of our fave events) have a new culinary fest (feast?) coming. Vegas Bites (Oct. 3, Palms Casino) invites some of our city’s most iconic restaurants to showcase their signature dish (think: the osso buco at Ferraro’s). A veritable buffet of our city’s most recognizable eateries and the plates that made them famous? Yes, please…

🥃 A well-respected Las Vegas whiskey brand that planned to open a distillery and tasting room near the Arts District has apparently shelved that idea. A “For Sale” sign has appeared on the property at the corner of Industrial and Wyoming avenues, raising the question: Is the distillery and tasting room moving, on hold, or DOA? Circle the (smoke) wagons…

🍔 While Bar Boheme (the restaurant) is taking this week off, Petite Boheme (the bar) remains open 🙌. Bad news: In Limbo Burgers (the bar’s Mon–Tue pop-up) disappears when Bar Boheme reopens Aug. 17. Good news: they’re serving burgers and dogs every day through Sunday, Aug. 16 — a fitting farewell to In Limbo’s fab food.

ORDER OF THE WEEK
All-Natural 1/2 Rotisserie Chicken + Beef Tallow Fries
Diamond Spur Provisions & Meats
​
Diamond Spur doesn’t do supermarket rotisserie chicken. Although the brand raises and sells its own beef, for chicken they turn to small family farms and partner ranches that mirror the Diamond Spur ethos of responsibly and sustainably raised meats. These gorgeous, non-GMO chickens are burnished, dripping, and slowly spun until the skin turns to glass. Get yours with the beef tallow fries — crisped in fat rendered from Diamond Spur’s Audubon Certified cattle, and just salty enough to make ketchup unnecessary. Half a chicken, full order of fries, one tray, zero regrets.
