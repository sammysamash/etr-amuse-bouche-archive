Sushi Sadness + Purse Pangs + Fall Fests
September 30, 2026 • Vol 1, Issue 35

the LEFTOVERS 🥡
🍣 Soon after opening in 2015, Yui Edomae Sushi gained a reputation for offering arguably the best sushi in Las Vegas. Sadly, it closed abruptly Saturday. The tiny Chinatown spot was beloved for its Tokyo-inspired omakase tastings featuring fresh raw fish served over standout seasoned rice. Fans of the omakase experience can still find it at Kabuto Edomae Sushi, which recently earned a Michelin Guide recommendation.
— ✦ —
☕ Le sigh. New York City’s Louis Vuitton flagship on 57th Street got a whole restaurant to keep its shoppers fed in style. Las Vegas? We’re getting a Coach coffee shop, stuffed into the Louis store at downtown's Premium Outlets. We get it: why splurge on filet mignon when a blueberry muffin will do? But with eight (‼️) Louis Vuitton stores in town, we’re feeling slighted.
— ✦ —
🍝 Readers know we’ve been tracking the happy hour comeback, but let's call this a happy dinner. Every Sunday (5–8:30pm), Monzù Italian Oven + Bar offers its Sunday Family Feast, a carb-lover’s dream that will fuel you through the work week. Just $29 per person (half off, 12 & under) gets endless rounds of chef Gio Mauro’s rectangular Roman-style pies (made with dough from a 300-year-old starter), plus a selection of house-made pastas and dessert. Enjoy live music and discounted wine, too. It’s not every day a restaurant of this caliber goes AYCE, so mangia while you can.
— ✦ —
🌮 Sadly, a number of local restaurants and bars have closed. Others, however, are not just surviving, but thriving. Take chef D. J. Flores, for example. In the nearly six years since opening Milpa Mexican Cafe, showcasing healthy, scratch-made food and heirloom corn tortillas, Flores has grown the operation to include two Arts District bars (Nocturno and Cobra Clutch), with a second Milpa and a tortilla chip company on the way. So while it's easy to blame bigger forces when restaurants and bars fail, we'd rather celebrate the wins.
— ✦ —
🍜 Speaking of… Shang Artisan Noodle — a locally-born restaurant regarded for its hand-pulled and knife-shaved noodles — is also growing quickly. First opened on W. Flamingo Rd in 2016, Shang’s fifth location opened in the northwest on Monday.​ This comes on the heels of an August opening near Downtown’s East Fremont district, plus at Durango’s food hall (2023) and Silverado Ranch (2024). Try the Shàng Beef Noodle soup.

SPILLED TEA 🫖

🥩 We mentioned that NYC’s highly-regarded American brasserie The Corner Store is coming to the Strip’s Cosmopolitan sometime later this year, but we didn’t say it’s owned by Catch Hospitality Group. Tilman Fertitta (who’s buying Caesars Entertainment) is 50% owner of CHG, leading us to wonder… Is this a move by Tilman a foothold for getting the Cosmo? 🤔
— ✦ —

🍸 We’re getting close to the opening of Arty’s (hopefully Oct 30…) and a potential kickstart to the rebirth of the historic (and notorious) Commercial Center. We won’t mention all the significant spots once there, but today the future is looking up. 🇹🇭 Lotus of Siam, a James Beard Award winner, recently reopened in its original home. 🍳 Vickie’s Diner (formerly Tiffany’s Cafe), relocated here in 2021. 🍚 Komol (and its sizable Thai vegetarian menu) has been a locals fave since 1986. Add Insomniac’s (Electric Daisy Carnival) planned $30 million investment into the Ice Palace/Sahara Event Center — the same room that hosted Led Zeppelin and Bob Marley! — and we’re cautiously optimistic that a “Commercial Center District” is on its way.
— ✦ —

☕ Dig It! Coffee, which hires adults of all ability levels, is opening a second location in the busy southwest, to complement its place in Symphony Park… 🥗 Rachel’s Kitchen is celebrating 20 years of healthy food, growing to six local spots, plus one in Frisco, TX… 👢 As Las Vegas embraces its country-western roots, Resorts World is leaping on the covered wagon with Miss Loretta’s, coming soon… 🥯 Saintwood, the mysterious southwest bagels and burgers joint, is now hiring, planning to open in October. Much like Significant Other (also opening in October), we hope the execution matches the marketing…

ORDER OF THE WEEK
Le Hamburger
Bar Boheme
​
It’s no secret that Eat Talk Repeat loves a good burger. Steakhouse–style, smashed, or grilled — if it's good, we’re crushing it. Our current obsession is the beauty you see above, from Bar Boheme. It's simply built, but don’t let that fool you. A thick ground beef patty (no smashing here), caramelized onions, cornichons, new-school American cheese and a swipe of dijonnaise add up to something nearly perfect. Sure, you can add a sunny-side egg or Nueske's crispy bacon, but we don't; the balance is that good. At happy hour (daily, 4–6pm), it’s a steal (and a meal) at $16 à la carte. The rest of the time, it’s $29 and includes a side of sensational frites fried in beef tallow, making it perfect for sharing. Régalez-vous!
