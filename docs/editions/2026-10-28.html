the LEFTOVERS 🥡
🌮 Family-founded and locally-born Al Vapor Street Tacos has added a fifth location to its rapidly growing Las Vegas footprint. Parked on the patio of The Palms’ non-smoking gaming area, the Al Vapor food truck offers a selection including its popular birria and asada tacos, quesadillas, nachos and fries, along with aguas frescas and the family’s signature salsas.
— ✦ —
🌴 Another taste of Hawaii is on its way to the Ninth Island: Penny’s Malasadas. Essentially a fried doughnut, malasadas were brought to Hawaii by Portuguese sugar plantation laborers in the late 1800s. Hawaiians made the treat their own by stuffing them with custard, pudding, and other fillings. The southwest Las Vegas location will be the brand’s second — and its first outside Hawaii.
— ✦ —
🇮🇹 Henderson residents barely had time to mourn the July exit of Tuscany Grill, a 27-year neighborhood stalwart on Eastern Ave, before its replacement, The Delmont, started to take shape. Billed on social media as "a place to eat, watch, play, and gather,” the new spot seems to have a split personality: Is it a gaming and sports bar? Or an Italian American Bistro? For now, we’ll celebrate the fact that they invested in a legit pizza oven, as we patiently await an anticipated fall opening.
— ✦ —
🐂 Yet Another butcher shop and deli, The Wise Ox, has opened in the southwest. Originating in southern California, the shop is a sister operation to the neighboring Rare Society steakhouse at Uncommons. This is the third Wise Ox location, and the first outside San Diego County. Both the butcher shop and the steakhouse are projects of chef Brad Wise.
— ✦ —
🥩 After a successful September 2024 pop-up dinner run at Wynn’s Tableau, Taylor Sheridan’s Four Sixes Ranch Steakhouse has found a steady Strip home… at MGM Grand. Originally thought to be locating permanently in Encore’s former Jardin space, Four Sixes’ move to MGM has spurred online speculation of behind-the-scenes drama (as if we didn't have enough of that, steakhouse-wise, this week). But Wynn-Encore is already stacked with SW Steakhouse and Sartiano’s Italian Steakhouse, while the vast MGM has only Tom Colicchio’s Craftsteak. With the MGM also hosting the PBR Team Series Championship at T-Mobile, as well as aggressively courting the Cowboy Contingent with NFR–themed events across its properties, perhaps it was simply just a matter of “fit.”

SPILLED TEA 🫖

🍺 Anyone wanting to operate a piece of (North) Las Vegas history might take a peek at the Hitchin’ Post Saloon. With roots reaching back to 1948, and once a bustling roadside relic of old Vegas, it now sits quiet with a “For Lease” banner hung above it. Opened in 1955 as a coffee shop serving the adjacent motel, it became the Convoy Club after landing a liquor license in 1977. Then in 1983, the Hitchin’ Post Saloon & Steakhouse was born. The bar is rumored to have been one of the first locally to have glycol-cooled beer taps, boasting the coldest draft in town.

🥃 We recently reported a “For Sale” sign posted on an old tour bus depot near the Arts District. The property (at the NW corner of Oakey and Industrial) was slated to become the headquarters for Nevada H&C Distilling, which produces the homegrown Smokewagon whiskey. The RJ reports that the parcel, purchased for $4+ million in 2023, is available for a cool $8+ million. Apparently, H&C needs a larger facility after landing a national distribution deal for its spirits.

ORDER OF THE WEEK
Beer Cheese Fondue
Las Vegas Brewing Co.
​
When she’s not hobnobbing in the rarified air of Zero Bond, Ash is often enjoying more accessible dining pleasures — with pretzels and beer among her faves. It’s no surprise, then, that she made her way a few blocks from the ETR headquarters to the Las Vegas Brewing Company. Tucked away on 3rd Street, LVBC remains a somewhat hidden Arts District gem.
​
The food menu is a tasty surprise here, featuring nine smashburger variations (including three vegan, if that’s your thing), a dirty hot dog, a brekkie sandwich (11am–1pm only), and yes, the Beer Cheese Fondue: two warm pretzel loaves and a sizable side of beer cheese for dipping. Wash it down with LVBC house brews from its Las Vegas brewery, select guest taps, a glass of bubbles 🥂, or a cocktail from the full bar 🥃.
