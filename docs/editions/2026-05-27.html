THE LEFTOVERS
🍸Commonwealth— a bar that accelerated East Fremont’s redevelopment nearly 14 years ago — recently underwent a “sympathetic restoration” leaving it fresh and familiar. The nightspot revitalized its downstairs bar with “new” vintage furnishings, crystal and Tiffany lighting, and 200 new pieces of art, all while maintaining the pre-Prohibition grit and glamour that make it a staple. The refresh extends to both its ensconced speakeasy (the lauded Laundry Room) and the upstairs deck. At ETR, we think this demonstrates how, unlike the Strip, existing Downtown venues do not require demolition or wholesale reinvention to remain “current.”
​
🍕Conjuring up the AYCE pizza buffets of our childhood, GiroGrano is bringing a pizza gorgefest to the Strip’s Miracle Mile Shops this fall. Fashioned after the familiar Brazilian rodizio steakhouse, GiroGrano servers will circulate the dining room with endless platters 🥴 of 28 styles of savory pizza (including curious selections like “Beef Stroganoff”) and 14 dessert versions — but we’ll save mentioning those for the podcast, when we can get John’s eye-roll filled reactions. While their concept has piqued our (read: Ash’s) interest, the AI-generated website has not.
​
🥐 Despite the rapidly rising success of Asian cuisine in Las Vegas, one world-renowned Japanese specialty-dessert shop has closed its last local location. Beard Papa’s, known for its customized, filled-to-order cream puffs, launched here in 2014 at a short-lived kiosk at The Linq. It eventually opened a southwest storefront on Rainbow Blvd, and one in the Town Square mall. All LV locations are now gone, which begs the question: Would such a spot have fared better in our foodie-centric Chinatown? 
​
🍹Speaking of Chinatown… the immersive, Golden Tiki has rolled out a fully-refreshed bar menu in advance of its 11th anniversary. It features a cheeky new selection of original tropical cocktails (“A Negroni Killed My Pet Lizard”) to complement the tiki classics, as well as some lighter snacks (Gochujang cucumbers marinated in Korean chili paste) to help keep the drinks flowing. What hasn’t changed is the bar's Disney-esque decor — thunderstorms, talking birds, swinging pirates — and unique DJ and live music events.
​
🍽️ Restaurant Week — the annual week-plus event — is back, plating special “dining for a cause” meals across the city. Participating restaurants create prix fixe menus, donating a portion of sales to Three Square Food Bank. Some ETR-approved local participants include: Amari Italian Kitchen ($60), High Steaks ($90), Aroma Latin American Cocina (lunch $40; dinner $80), Chyna Club ($100), Holstein’s (lunch & dinner $40), Partage ($110), Lamaii (lunch $40; dinner $50). Sparrow + Wolf ($100), Hayworth ($80), Main St. Provisions ($70), Milpa (lunch & dinner $30), Nocturno Cocktail Bar ($50), Other Mama ($60), Wineaux ($50), Winnie & Ethel’s (brunch $30), and Soul Belly BBQ ($30, lunch only). SPILLED TEA

A locally-born Greek fast-casual eatery has been teasing its new south-end Arts District location on Commerce Ave for some time now, and we couldn't be more excited. Unfortunately, construction appears to have been temporarily halted, despite the build-out appearing nearly complete. Whispers suggest the contractor has stepped back while awaiting payment, ​bringing what was an impeding June opening date into question… 
​
Apparently, everyone is a little short on funds. Luckily, in steps a locally-built, crowd-sourced hub that tracks Las Vegas happy hours in real time. BluebirdLV leans on user submissions to maintain a live-updated database of drink and food specials across the valley. Instead of digging through outdated lists or the interrupted algorithm of social posts, users can see what’s really happening right now — and contribute updates as deals shift or new ones arise. During a moment when Las Vegas prices are front and center, Bluebird is built for locals who want to maximize their budget without playing food detective each time they go out. Let us know if it’s worth the hype!

ORDER OF THE WEEK
House Fried Fat Noodles (w/ Shrimp!)
@Shanghai Taste
​
Craving a tasty noodle nosh from the alleys of Shanghai? These House Fried Fat Noodles are satisfyingly thick and chewy strands of wheat pasta, wok-tossed until they absorb the deep, slightly smoky flavor that only high heat can create. They’re available with pork, chicken, or beef, too, but the shrimp brings a light sweetness to balance the savory sauce coating every bite. It’s pure comfort food in every bite.
​
ETR Pro Tip: These noodles are a great leftover item, as we are convinced they just might be slightly better the next day!
