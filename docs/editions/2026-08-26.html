Fundraisers, a European Cafe & a (new) DTLV Brasserie
August 26, 2026 • Vol 1, Issue 30

the LEFTOVERS
👩‍🚒 ​When the sirens came — and kept coming — right outside our Arts District office, we feared something terrible was happening. Sadly, just blocks away, a fire severely damaged two local businesses. Now, the community is stepping up with a duo of Sep 3 fundraisers for Raw Remedies and Analog Dope. At Echo Taste & Sound, one of our fave spots, enjoy Chef Nat’s $50 AYCE fried chicken dinner and fixins. Or there's a $20 version at Dustland — same chicken, with a beer. Either benefits both businesses. 
​
☕ Here’s a feel-good story from Downtown: Nick Della Penna, an owner-founder of the Great Greek restaurants across Las Vegas, leased a vacant coffeehouse across from the Las Vegas Academy​ of the Arts to open The Muse Cafe. He plans to integrate student life and studies into its design and operations — think live performances, student-run social media accounts, and a documentary about the café’s opening. Nick says he will donate the profits (he’s aiming for $100k a year 🤑) to the fundraising arm of LVA. Muse should open mid-October.
​
🇮🇹 Speaking of coffee… despite all the various versions of third-wave coffee and Asian-inspired tea across the valley, we at ETR are thrilled that an Italian-style coffeehouse has arrived. Torino Café embraces the European café vibe — a place to grab an espresso and run, or stop, sip, and chat — and showcases a Turin fave, the Bicerin. Sure, John is mad that it’s in Henderson, but we’ll drag him there soon.
​
🍧 Of all the imports coming to Las Vegas this one feels most natural for the Ninth Island: the Original Big Island Shave Ice Co. Founded nearly 70 years ago, OBISC is Hawaii's O.G.. Even more special? The LV store will be its first beyond its current location in Waikoloa on the Big Island. 
​
🥩 Downtown is getting an outpost of Boom Bang Fine Foods & Cocktails, the Palm Springs–styled Henderson brasserie from Top Chef alum Elia Aboumrad. She and her team are taking a space at the new Civic Plaza, across from City Hall, later this year. Given all those captive employees, lunch should take care of itself. Dinner will be harder. Our hope? They use their cocktail program to help resurrect the office happy hour ​🕔.
 

SPILLED TEA

🇪🇸 When it comes to the new location of Valencian Gold (chef Jeffrey Weiss’s tapas restaurant being reborn in the Arts District) an anonymous reader muses: “If we are taking bets, I think Valencian Gold is going in the former Berlin space. It has a kitchen (needs an upgrade though), bar, intimate space, [and] great patio potential.” It also has a downtown rarity — its own parking lot. 🤔

🧐 Speaking of Chef Weiss… He sat down with us for this week’s Eat Talk Repeat ​🎧 and casually dropped that he’s opening more restaurants. Our money’s on a Mark Marrone (of Diamond Spur) collab…​

👀 While there have been several Arts District closings this year, the struggles reach beyond the ’hood. The Bonfire — a rock and roll bar in the same Paradise Rd complex as the long-running Office Bar and Buffalo-style Naked City Pizza — has announced it will shutter after service Aug 30. This comes just over two years after Bonfire assumed the space where FreeZone served the LGBTQ community for decades…

🍸 Espresso martinis have the current zeitgeist in a headlock, much to the chafing of John's sensibilities. KNPR’s Desert Companion recently rounded up some of our city's better versions, including a tequila-based number at Bourbon Steak. A notable omission: Piero's, which pulls a fresh shot of espresso for theirs, and is a fave of one ETR staffer…

🥔 Dolly Parton — who passed away 💔​ at 80 just as this Amuse-Bouche was being finished — once shared a fun food fact with Today: She loved potatoes. Baked, mashed, fried — she loved them all, eating some every day. There’s something wonderful in a superstar being happiest with one of the most humble foods. We'll pour a few frites out for you tonight, Dolly…

ORDER OF THE WEEK
The Montreal Classic
Lester’s Deli
​
If you think the fight over the best pizza (or burger) is a barn-burner, you’ve never watched two people go to war over pastrami. Lester's Deli sidesteps the whole debate by not serving pastrami at all — this is Montreal-style sliced brisket, cured with an old-world spice blend, smoked, steamed, and piled onto seeded rye with a smear of yellow mustard. It doesn't come cheap ($21 regular, $27 overstuffed), but for a smoked meat sando this tasty and tender — and sizable enough to split — we’re willing to get spendy. In a nod to authenticity, the new Henderson outpost (only the second one worldwide) is co-owned by Montreal native and Cirque musical director Jean-Francois Blais. For now, Lester’s is in soft-open mode: shorter menu and hours (10am–4pm), closed Mondays. A full menu and longer hours should arrive in September.
