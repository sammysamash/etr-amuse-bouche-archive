THE LEFTOVERS
🍨 Ice cream is food. Fight us 🥊. To recognize the tasty treat in true Las Vegas fashion, Area15 is opening what will be (of course) the world's largest ice cream museum. Come July 3, you’ll have thirty thousand square feet in which to fro-lick 🙄 and celebrate your love of the creamy cousin of ​gelato. An AYCE ice cream buffet! A tattoo​parlor! A wedding chapel! A vintage Vegas supper club! We're not sure how some of this relates to ice cream, but we are certain that Area15 is taking the experiential economy to places we never imagined. It’s been a minute since we last got sick from eating too much ice cream, but that buffet might be the answer…
​
🍎🍏 What goes better with ice cream than apple pie? While this may not tickle John's fancy pants, our nostalgia is amped up for a (brief) return of the legendary McDonald's Apple Pie, Fried Version. Once upon a time (pre-1992 — cue the Dad Rock), McD’s pies (they had cherry then, too) were deep-fried in sizzling beef tallow and served hot as molten lava. While now fried in palm oil, the pies are available through July 4th… because nothing says “America” like a roof-of-the-mouth blister.
​
🍣 A newish southwest sushi spot is gaining traction, thanks to its presentation and precise plating, plus a reputation for freshness derived from nearly three years operating out of a 200SF ghost kitchen. Doanburi opened in January, with chef Jae Doan at the knives. She has the pedigree — training under Masaharu Morimoto, working as sushi sous chef at Mizumi — and an eye for detail, even crafting soy sauce in house. Kanpai!
 

SPILLED TEA 

A beloved Honolulu-born Hawaiian diner quickly launched five LV locations starting in 2023, spreading local love for loco moco far beyond the Starboard Tack. But Zippy's, which recently opened in the Charleston Blvd spot that once housed Islands Burgers 😔, quickly announced the June 28 closure of its South Summerlin location, just 15 months after opening. No matter how popular you (think you) are, there are limits to the ohana…

While the churn of a southwest location can be difficult, chef Marc Marrone’s recently-opened Diamond Spur Provisions & Meats is on 🔥 out there. Whispers over the grill suggest DSPM is already pushing expansion — first in Sturgis, South Dakota, then a second location here. So popular is the butcher-deli that a planned media day Wednesday had to be postponed because, um, they ran out of provisions 🤯…

Not everything that hopes to f̶l̶e̶e̶c̶e̶ serve Las Vegas tourists is a guaranteed money-maker — not even an established out-of-state bar brand that dropped $50 million on a new Strip-front building. Bottled Blonde, a multi-story sports bar hailing from Scottsdale, has shifted gears after less than a year here, handing over daily operations by “entering into a strategic partnership with a Las Vegas-based hospitality powerhouse.” That's a lot of words for, “Uh, we need help.”…

ORDER OF THE WEEK
Green Vegan Dumplings
@Shanghai Taste
​
This Chinatown staple is well known for its amazing Xiao Long Bao soup dumplings, and for good reason. But the secret worth knowing, especially if you are seeking something lighter (or, less 🐷 porky), are the Green Vegan Dumplings. Filled with house-made and smoked organic minced tofu, vermicelli noodles, and bok choy, these meatless wonders are tender, savory, and satisfying. Don’t worry; the traditional pork dumplings will always be there.
