Openings & Closings: The Vegas Churn Continues
August 05, 2026 • Vol 1, Issue 27

the LEFTOVERS
🇯🇵 A couple weeks back, we teased a new concept taking over the bar at the Arts District location of Houston Hot Chicken. It launches tonight at 5pm with the opening of Cobra Clutch — a cocktail collab from chef D.J. Flores (Milpa) and beverage director Lu Lopez (Nocturno). Along with the expected adventurous drinks 🍹 menu, the enticing hangout is inspired by Japanese city-pop, with a hyper-detailed interior featuring old school arcade games and classic films. The limited seating and no reservations policy likely means an outdoor waiting line 🥵 from the get-go.
​
🌮 Las Vegas in experiencing a superbloom of spots serving high-quality, low-key foods — the kind John hates to love. Now, Station Casinos has lured (yet another) SoCal-approved​ taco spot to town. Villa’s Tacos is no ordinary roadside shack, arriving to Durango Casino with a  “Bib Gourmand” Michelin Guide thumbs-up, a Super Bowl halftime show cameo, and an origin story from grandma’s front yard. Chef-owner Victor Villa brings his full Highland Park lineup — homemade blue corn tortillas, wood-fired al pastor, ranchera asada, queso tacos, mulitas — to the Eat Your Heart Out food hall Aug. 15, taking over the previous Fiorella location.
​
3️⃣ How do you feel about a restaurant specializing in coffee​ ☕ + bagels​ 🥯 +​ burgers​ 🍔 ? The new Saintwood Cafe — a semi-mysterious concept planning to open near Jones and Russell in southwest Las Vegas this fall — hopes you feel intrigued. We do. From what we’ve seen, we can't help but feel a Bukowski-meets-Bourdain vibe — though we hope the kitchen is a step ahead of their web team, who (as of this writing) misspelled their name on their website. 🫣
​
🍔 One more burger note, with apologies. Remember Pats Patty’s (yes, that’s how they spell it) — the food-truck borne smashburger joint that closed its Tropicana Ave storefront in January? They aim to reopen “soon” at Flamingo Rd and I-215. The brainchild of chef Ryan Fox, Pats uses 100% Snake River Farms American Wagyu to make their signature Okie burgers (a regional fave smashed​ directly into a massive heap of thinly sliced onions), with fries cooked in Wagyu fat.😋 Speaking of fat, is Vegas saturated 🙄 with burgers yet?

SPILLED TEA 

Boca Park’s Sambalatte Torrefazione, an original hangout for Vegas coffee connoisseurs (and likely the city’s first “Third Wave” coffeehouse), closes Aug. 27. Unable to reach a new lease agreement, founder Luiz Olivera says he hopes to reopen the groundbreaking cafe, which once had multiple valley locations, including at the Smith Center (which is now a…Starbucks 😑).

Arts District watering hole Stray Pirate — the dog-themed tropical cocktail bar — has connected with Hera The Dog Vodka and SPCA for the Swig & Berries charity cocktail. A portion of each cocktail sale directly supports the SPCA’s no-kill animal shelter 🐶 so go get some…

Lilli, the acclaimed Durango Social Club pop-up from French Laundry alum Tyler Vorce, has a permanent home. Vorce has taken over the Chinatown spot vacated by Le Café du Vegas, targeting a summer opening…

Nittaya’s Secret Kitchen will soon have a second location near the Strip, taking over the short-lived Bramàre at The Collective on Paradise Rd. Already packed with notable eateries (Braseria, Cleaver, Marrakesh), the location presents chef-owner Nittaya Parawong an opportunity to capture the lucrative convention market, as well as diners from nearby high-rises and the Las Vegas Country Club…

WEEKLY PET PEEVE

I really hate it when a restaurant reserves bar seating. Eating at a bar offers a lot of advantages (dining alone, lighter meals). It also gives people a chance to walk up and wait for a seat without having to score a coveted (or impossible to get) table reservation. Managers, open bar seating doesn't have to be a chaotic free-for-all… serve me a drink and put me on a wait list!

–SDL
 

ORDER OF THE WEEK
Cordon Bleu Sandwich
Bar Boheme
​
Can there be anything more French than a chicken cordon bleu sandwich and a glass of bubbles on a lazy Sunday? Available only at weekend brunch, this incredible bite from chef Sean O'Hara showcases classic French ingredients — Jidori chicken, Gruyère cheese, Mornay sauce, jambon de Paris — all deliciously composed on a fresh-baked milk bun. The twice-fried beef tallow frites are exactly what fries should be, while the sweet cut of the champagne resets the palate for another bite. Divine.
