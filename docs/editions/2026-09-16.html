Coffee, Cocktails, Croissants & Closures
September 16, 2026 • Vol 1, Issue 33

the LEFTOVERS
🛫 With the October opening of Significant Other Kitchen & Cocktails, the Airport District (we just made that up) will get its second posh restaurant — about 24 years (‼️) after Panevino landed down the road. Locals Mike and Kristi Austin enlisted restaurateur Matthew Silverman (Vintner Grill), who corralled chef Matthew Piekarski. The Austins hope to entice locals — as well as travelers on layovers, the business crowd, and remote workers — with a three-meal menu and a pup-friendly patio. Menus are not yet available, but the website shares a list of modern brasserie hitmakers — house-made pasta! dry-aged steaks! carefully selected seafood! — plus the (expected) burger and the (unexpected) nori rolls. That website is undeniably beautiful; October will tell us if the food and service live up to the HTML.
​
☕ Good news for fans of Sambalatte, arguably the city’s original third-wave coffeehouse, which had six locations around the valley. After recently closing its final (and flagship) storefront in Boca Park, the espresso cafe has resurfaced down the block at Tivoli Village. Sharing space with PKWY Tavern, Sambalatte’s Espresso Urbano is a compact but familiar version of the community coffeehouse Las Vegas enjoyed for 16 years.
​
🌮 Aroma Latin Cocina is still settling into its new digs next to Sand Dollar Lounge (which, incidentally, serves a darn good bar pizza). After briefly testing lunch, dinner, and a late-night pop-up or two, Aroma is shifting to nighttime hours only (dinner Tue–Sat, 5–9pm; late-night Fri–Sat, 9pm–1am) — a move that makes sense in Chinatown. Chef Steve Kestler has created a 20-item late-night menu that includes eight varieties of tacos, ceviche, a Cuban sandwich, a Parrillada Para for two, plus post-boozing faves like a Perro Caliente 🌭 and Carne Cruda Fries.
​
​👩‍🍳 Picnic in the Alley, an epicurean event that raises money for the Women’s Hospitality Initiative, returns to the Las Vegas Arts District on Sunday, October 25th after a year at a suburban farm. The food and drink tasting showcases women in hospitality, from chefs to beverage pros. Tickets are available now, and VIP tends to sell quickly, so get yours soon. As the organizers say, “come for the food, stay for the movement.”
​
🍔 Stay Tuned — the Vegas-born smashburger sensation — is teasing a new storefront on its Instagram. Started as a COVID-era backyard pop-up, Stay Tuned has been serving from the kitchen at the Hard Hat Lounge for nearly three years. Although the specific spot for location #2 has not been announced, they do admit in the Instagram comments that it’s in Henderson.

SPILLED TEA

💰 We knew the Las Vegas Civic Plaza’s only full-service bar and restaurant would be an outpost of Henderson fave Boom Bang Fine Food & Cocktails. What we didn't know is that it's being built with a $1 million cash infusion from the City of Las Vegas. Of that, $300k is repayable; the other $700k is a tenant improvement allowance toward a buildout the City keeps if Boom Bang leaves.​ Opening Summer 2027, Boom Bang has us (and the City…) hoping it can revitalize the area’s post-work dining scene.

🍕 It's a good sign (and a rare one) when the chef behind the restaurant is working the line at a new location. Recently, we spied Good Pie founding pizzaiolo Vincent Rotolo at the ovens of his new location at Red Rock Casino, which probably explains why Good Pie anywhere is usually great…

🔐 Two restaurants have quietly closed… “temporarily” 🤔. Yu-Or-Mi opened in 2020 as a quality sushi spot in the Arts District, then shifted to a pan-Asian menu in October after its sushi program moved to the Durango Casino. Yelp says Yu-Or-Mi will reopen in December, but it’s apparently listed for lease as a “fully built” restaurant that’s “ready for a new concept” …

🇮🇹 And Roma Deli, a popular hole-in-the-wall Italian deli and restaurant in Chinatown since 1999, has also (“temporarily”) closed. Online listings are conflicted, but recent drive-bys by the ETR team confirm it’s not open. We’ll watch both locations as things develop…

🪩 A louder closure: While Las Vegas mourns the curtain dropping on several Strip stage shows this year, some are mourning a restaurant. Diner Ross, the Spiegelworld spot built alongside its high-energy DiscoShow 🕺 (which took its final spin in January), closes after service Sep. 26, taking the 99 Prince and Glitterloft bars with it. Turns out the immersively themed diner and bars couldn’t keep the party going without the show 😔.

🥐​ We told you that the Bar Boheme Bakery pops up on the 3rd Saturday each month. Apparently, however, it’s scheduled on the 3rd weekend on the same day as Market in the Alley. This month, the market (and the bakery) falls on Sunday, Sep. 20. Confused? Us, too… but we’ll still be there.

ORDER OF THE WEEK
Smoked Turkey Club Sandwich
1228 Main
​
It could be because it's still summer, but the sandwiches just keep coming. This one — the Smoked Turkey Club at 1228 Main — is deceptive in that its apparent simplicity hides some genuinely complex and satisfying layers of flavor. It arrives on house-baked ciabatta spread with jalapeño aioli, then stacked with smoked turkey, crisp bacon, thick slices of never-bitter heirloom tomato, and baby romaine. While nothing on that ingredients list surprises you, eating it will. The house-made chips? They’re the bonus.
