Beer Bars (+ Spas), Buckle Bars & Bagels
November 04, 2026 • Vol 1, Issue 40

the LEFTOVERS 🥡
🍺 🧖🏻‍♀️ We like beer (well, except John). We like relaxing at spas (well, some of us do). What we aren't sure about is a $1 million​ spa ​that serves beer​ moving into the corner of Main St at Charleston Blvd. Hailing from Denver (maybe they should consider The Colorado building down the street?), Oakwell Beer Spa hopes to bring some of that city’s Rocky Mountain wellness energy to the Arts District. We’re not saying we’re skeptical; we’ll even give it a try (well, some of us will). We’re just saying that a million-dollar spa on a street known for $10 tattoos, $3 beers and $2 mystery shots is either a sign of where the Arts District is headed, a sign that it’s already arrived, or a sign that someone is about to take a million-dollar (beer) bath.
— ✦ —
🎺 Las Vegas once had a vibrant live jazz scene, owing itself to the musicians who worked in Strip orchestras that often played six nights a week (hence, “Mondays Dark”). Today, the scene is in revival, with multiple spots hosting live music, drinks and food. The stalwart Dispensary Lounge​ has had Thursday jazz nights (and a tasty burger) for much of its 50 years, while relative newcomer Vic’s ​(2023) prides itself on an Italian supper club vibe and exceptional acoustics for its local and national acts. Other spots include the long-running Bootlegger Bistro (Italian), Maxan Jazz (sushi), and, of course, the Italian American Club. None of these places will necessarily blow away your palate, but you will enjoy a taste of what Las Vegas was like before digital audio undercut the Musicians' Union.
— ✦ —
🥯 Las Vegas’s longstanding (1996) schmear-slinging king of westside bagel breakfasts, Bagel Cafe, is about to get some competition. Bagelmania is preparing to open its new Boca Park location, alongside local sister brand Pinkbox Doughnuts. Opened as Harry's Bagelmania in 1989, developer Stephen Siegel bought the deli in 2018, reportedly after the elderly founders offered it to him during his Sunday morning bagel run. While not everyone is happy about the brand's sale, in 2021, Siegel moved the deli into a flashy flagship location on Convention Center Drive, taking all the recipes (and much of the staff) with him. Along with Bagelmania and Pinkbox, Siegel also purchased a majority ownership of Piero's Italian Cuisine from aging founder Freddie Glusman in 2025, after a family dispute left Glusman without an operational successor.
— ✦ —
🐎 With the National Finals Rodeo takeover less than four weeks away, Las Vegas suddenly boasts a corral full of country-western saloons vying for the attention of nearly 200,000 cowboys and 'girls expected to trot into town. Gilley’s and stalwart Stoney’s (now with three locations) have been joined in recent years by Ole Red, Jason Aldean's Kitchen & Bar, and, most recently, Luke Combs’ Category 10 — each promising its own twist on the flashy roadhouse trend. For those seeking a classic Vegas honky-tonk vibe and willing to take the F-650 for a drive, ETR suggests The Hideaway. One of our “50 Essential Bars,” this 24/7 institution hails from 1962, boasts live music, a polished wood dance floor, and serves up a pretty good chili burger.

SPILLED TEA 🫖

🍺 Some surprising (but as-yet-unconfirmed) whispers in the Arts District say that our favorite beer bar has been sold. The celebrated and award-winning Silver Stamp (which is nothing if not a visit to your Midwest uncle's basement, circa 1978) has reportedly been sold to the owners of neighboring Atomic Tavern. Those owners also happen to own the property where Atomic Tavern, Silver Stamp and a barbershop reside, plus they operate Atomic Liquors on Fremont East…
— ✦ —

🍸 With a chill finally in the air, this drink looks delicious 😋. Don’t feel like bartending for yourself? Juyoung Kang of Doberman Drawing Room has released its new “Symphony” cocktail menu, showcasing four signature cocktails ($20 each); the $28, two-pour “Odette and Odile” (based on the black and white swans of “Swan Lake”); and the “Four Seasons” $16 zero-proof drinks. Some may call it pretentious; we prefer civilized.
— ✦ —

🍕 The cohort sometimes called “Vegas Kids” (those who grew up here before The Mirage opened) may not know the name Carmine Vento, but they surely know his Villa Pizza. Sadly, Vento died last week at 78. Credited with bringing New York slices to Las Vegas, Villa Pizza first opened in Commercial Center in 1976. His slices then became the lunch of choice for a generation of mall kids when The Meadows opened in 1978. In recent years, Vento and his family opened four Carmine's Pizza Kitchen spots around the valley. Arrivederci, Carmine. And grazie.

Both add cost or reduce sales, and offset the reasons restaurants and bars can offer happy hour menus. BITE CLUB: Breakfast Banger
Mediterranean Style Omelet
1228 Main
​
While we have strong opinions about 1228 Main (Bring back dinner service! Bring back the chicken pot pie!), it’s only because we love the place. And one thing we really love is their Mediterranean Style Omelet. Fashioned from cage-free eggs, goat cheese, tomatoes, olives and arugula, this delicately-folded breakfast treat offers a protein punch packed with flavor.
​
The tangy goat cheese and briny olives give it a little Greek-isle sunshine, and the peppery arugula brightens every bite. Since opening in the Arts District in 2023 (with Wolfgang Puck as a partner), 1228 Main has built its reputation on house-baked breads​ and​ pastries —​ including our​Croissant Crawl​ winner. So do yourself a favor and grab something from the bakery counter on the way out. The rum-kissed banana bread makes a pretty convincing dessert. Or, office treat.
