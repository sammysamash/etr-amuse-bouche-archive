Coffee, Cocktails & Wine: Three of our Favorite Things
August 19, 2026 • Vol 1, Issue 29

the LEFTOVERS
🍸 Happy Hours are all the rage among the non-ascot wearing​ crowd, and one of the latest has even piqued our interest. Mae Daly’s — Vegas’ Best Steakhouse​ In An​ Unlikely Location — has officially entered the chat. Their deal is simple: $12 cocktails, half-off wine by-the-glass 🍷, and $12 ​for their​ cheeseburger (with fries) 🍔. This burger at that price is theft — it’s regularly $35 and​ worth every penny. Trust us. It placed 3rd in the ETR Steakhouse Burger Crawl — behind Delmonico Steakhouse and Peter Luger, and above the $52 version at Nicco’s (we tasted 16). Mae Daly’s happy hour is built for a post-work wind-down, running 5–7pm, Tu–Th.
​
☕​ Local coffeehouses are brewing up new spots as demand grows (we assume) for their caffeinated vibes and nommable nosh. Makers & Finders — an Arts District original that has grown to three locations — will soon open a fourth at the Fashion Show Mall kiosk that previously housed a Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf. And our neighbor, Vesta Coffee Roasters (another DTLV O.G.), is at the starting line of opening their fifth spot, this one in the burgeoning southwest valley.
​
🍹 Meanwhile, one coffee pour-veyor 🙄 is turning toward booze, presumably to capture the overlap between baristas and bartenders (one pours you into your day, one pours you out…). Slated to open this month, Terminal 4 craft cocktail bar commands the second floor of Kyle Cunningham’s sixth Coffee Class location, already serving on Water St. So along with Grey Witch, does this make two craft cocktail bars in Henderson?
​
🍇 Fans of Boulder City’s closed wine bar Milo’s Cellar know that Marcello Mauro​ of the busy Mauro family (Nora’s, Monzu, Old School Pizzeria)​ purchased the location from Milo Hurst’s family after Hurst’s death almost two years ago. Work has now begun to transform it to Nora’s Lakeside — a B.C. outpost of their popular Flamingo Rd restaurant bearing the matriarch’s name. Due to the extensive remodel required (including an expanded kitchen), construction is expected to take about a year. 
 

SPILLED TEA 

🌜 When we toured the (current) Esther’s Kitchen during build-out, Chef-owner James Trees teased us with late night food service. That never materialized ☹️ but down the block at Petite Boheme, late-night grub is a reality. Now serving 13 Bar Boheme faves (burger, poutine, flat iron steak, French onion grilled cheese) nightly to 1am, Petite will help fill a need for quality after-hours nosh — a Vegas tradition hard to find since shutdown…

🤑 Apparently Generation Z never got the etiquette memo. The good news? The Kids are apparently going out​ and drinking again. The bad news? They aren’t tipping, so when the swivel happens, they select “Other” and enter $0. This apparently confirms that Millennials aren’t the worst generation, despite how much heat they’ve taken over the years…

👀 Thanks to a fan tip 😉 we learned that El Yaquesito — a food truck/cart serving Sonoran seafood tostadas, tacos, and a shrimp and tuna fin soup called cahuamanta — scored a spot on the New York Times list, “41 of the Greatest Mexican Restaurants Across the United States.” We haven’t tried it, but wonder how the Times found it among all the other “obvious” outstanding Mexican food here. If you’ve been, let us know what you think…

ORDER OF THE WEEK
Double-Double + Fries & a Pup Patty
In-N-Out Burger
​
There probably isn't a single person in the southwestern U.S. who doesn't recognize the iconic California-style griddle burger from In-N-Out — vegetarians included. Born in Baldwin Park in 1948, In-N-Out has remained fiercely devoted to fresh ingredients, made-to-order food, and actual customer service for 77 years and counting. The photo above is all of that on one tray: “light-well fries,” a “Double-Double with light spread, add pickles,” and a seasoning-free Pup Patty for Rocky. Despite all the attention (and price bumps) on burgers this year, we’re happy to report that a $7 burger this fresh and consistently yummy still beats most of the pricier ones.
