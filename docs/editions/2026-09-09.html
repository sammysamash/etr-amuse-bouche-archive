New Eats: Chinatown, Summerlin & Downtown
September 09, 2026 • Vol 1, Issue 32

the LEFTOVERS
🇫🇷 Chinatown continues to assert itself as Las Vegas's premiere dining neighborhood. Now comes Lilli, the former pop-up from chef Tyler Vorce, which opens in a new permanent home on October 8. Lilli will continue the pop-up’s tradition of mixing French technique, seasonal American ingredients, and Mediterranean influences. Along with the prix fixe and tasting menus found in the dining room, Lilli will also have a lounge offering a more casual à la carte food menu, cocktails, and wine.
​
🍳 Staying in the neighborhood, it looks like a new three-meal eatery is coming to Shanghai Plaza… eventually. Although information is sparse, the temporary sign above Oak & Yolk says it will serve breakfast by day, and turn into a grill at night. The breakfast-all-day fans among us will keep an eye on this as it develops.
​
🍜 We’re not shy to admit it: one of our fave parts of Asian noodle soups like ramen and pho is the broth. So when we hear that a new place sells ramen without broth, it gives us pause. Instead, Nagaiki Ramen uses fats and oils to coat the abura soba noodles (think butter or oil on an Italian noodle dish). We’re adventurous, we just aren’t convinced — yet.
​
🍺 Sliding over to Summerlin, Finney's — the southern California brewpub we said would open its first Las Vegas location ​in the previous Wolfgang Puck Player's Locker — has announced a Sep 21 date. Along with "approachable dishes" like bowls, wings, pizzas and burgers, Finney's will have a dog-friendly terrace 🐶 and 20 hi-def TVs 😑. Can we normalize not watching screens all the time?
​
🌃 Heading downtown… Is the El Cortez taking a cue from Oak & Yolk? You may recall that the casino’s three-meal Siegel's 1941 closed, to be transformed into the dinner-only Alex Prime Steakhouse. Either our info was wrong, or the El Cortez altered its plans. What’s announced now: the remodeled restaurant will reopen in the fall with a dual personality. During daytime, Siegel’s 1941 serves breakfast and lunch. At night, it becomes Alex Prime. Honestly, we're just excited the Prime Rib special returns for lunch.

SPILLED TEA

🐟​ While we always default to Ole Manny's, there’s some sad news for westside/Summerlin fans of SoCal fish taco chain Wahoo’s, which recently closed its Boca Park location after 14 years. Maybe it was hard to compete with the viral success of another SoCal fave, The Taco Stand, just across the street. One advantage? Wahoo’s has a full bar. Other locations remain open, including one serving food 24/7 near the Palms…

🍕 When we saw that Robbie Cunningham's delish downtown Detroit pizza spot Guerrilla Pizza was adding sandwiches, we were intrigued. When we learned he was baking sandwich bread using his primo pizza dough, we were sold. From what we can see, Cunningham uses entire loaves sliced lengthwise, and the resulting monster sando is like something Fred Flintstone would make for himself. The restaurant currently offers three varieties (Turkey, Garlic Parm Chicken, Italian) for about $15 each… 

🍎​ And finally, Friday will be 25 years since the attacks on the World Trade Center in NYC. Longtime local restaurateur Steven Montesano is marking the date by offering a dozen bagels for $9.11 at his New York Bagel & Bakery in the Medical District. Yep, it’s a good deal, but the real value comes from discovering a spot that takes its New York City heritage seriously…

ORDER OF THE WEEK
The Belly of Soul
SoulBelly BBQ
​
Regular readers know that Ash has been on a sando kick of late. We're trying to get her over to Guerilla Pizza Co for some new flavors, but in the meantime, one of her go-to’s is the Belly of Soul at chef Bruce Kalman's​SoulBelly BBQ, smoking in the Arts District since 2021.
​
Piled high with house-smoked brisket, a fried egg, cheese sauce, pickled onions, spicy barbecue sauce, and crispy onions, this BBQ behemoth gives plenty of people pause when dropped at the table. The only way to go at it is to just go at it. Tuck a napkin in your shirt, grab with both hands, and open wide — no shame, no regrets.
